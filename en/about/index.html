About.

I work at the intersection of university research, applied research, and education. I have spent more than twenty years in computer vision, working on 3D reconstruction and image and video analysis, and now on spatial intelligence for engineering and robotics.

Previous positions

Samsung Research Center LLC

May 2018 – February 2024
Organization page↗
  • 2021–2024

    Head of AI (Samsung AI Center Moscow)

    Led 53 engineers, two laboratories, and 11 projects spanning 3D scene recognition and reconstruction, object modeling from photographs, interactive and semantic segmentation, multitask models, realistic avatars, and image and video generation. Staff attrition fell from over 50% to 25% in two years; the center produced 13 papers and 8 patents in 2023.

  • 2018–2020

    Head of the Visual Understanding Lab

    Led a team of 15 working on 3D vision for a home robot: SLAM and the construction and analysis of semantic 3D maps. The team prepared a five-year plan, a SLAM benchmark, system components, and a robot prototype, and published two papers at IROS 2019. After a change in company strategy, the lab shifted to image editing and 3D scene reconstruction from smartphone photos.
