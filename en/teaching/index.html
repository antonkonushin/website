Teaching and mentoring.

Defended doctoral dissertations and playlists of recorded courses.

Over more than 20 years of teaching, I have supervised 9 PhD graduates and more than 110 students working on their degree theses. I developed the Computer Vision course, which I taught at the MSU Faculty of Computational Mathematics and Cybernetics, the Yandex School of Data Analysis, and HSE University. Through this course, I recruited all the students I have supervised for research, received the ML Prize in 2024, and was inducted into the ML Prize Hall of Fame in 2026.

  • Lomonosov Moscow State University

    Since 2026, I have headed the master's and PhD programs at the MSU Faculty of AI, which I helped establish. Before that, I worked and taught for many years at the MSU Faculty of Computational Mathematics and Cybernetics.

  • Yandex School of Data Analysis

    From 2009 to 2026, I taught “Computer Vision”, then “3D Computer Vision” and “Multimodal LLMs”.

  • HSE University

    I helped establish HSE University's Faculty of Computer Science in 2014. I was academic director of the bachelor's program in Applied Mathematics and Computer Science from 2014 to 2019, and research director of the program from 2019 to 2025.
